# Brand and Product Language

## Name

The product name is **Concert Passport** in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. It is a proper name and is not translated into three visually competing logos.

The descriptor adapts to language:

- English: `K-pop shows, in one place`
- Simplified Chinese: `K-pop 演出，一站管理`
- Traditional Chinese: `K-pop 演出，一站管理`

## Mark

The repository-owned mark is a circular impression crossed by one continuous route-like waveform and finished by a periwinkle terminal point. The circle can read as a record, an arena seen from above, or a passport stamp; the cut line joins live music and travel without drawing a literal ticket, plane, pin, or artist lightstick. It contains no initials, airline shorthand, artist reference, or ticket-platform trademark.

The circular route is the primary mark. It is paired with the `Concert Passport` wordmark only where there is enough horizontal space. The terminal uses the product accent while the impression follows the surrounding foreground colour. At app-icon scale the mark sits on an ink field with a warm bone impression and periwinkle terminal. Do not place the mark inside another outline, add a gradient, or turn it into a decorative badge.

## Visual language

Concert Passport uses an editorial layout, strong typography, generous negative space, hairline dividers, and a restrained iris action color. Light and dark appearances share the same hierarchy. Dense operational information is expressed through rows, timelines, and typographic rhythm rather than nested rounded cards.

Selection never relies on a coloured edge rail or a long underline. Global navigation uses weight plus one four-pixel point; data selection uses a quiet surface change; filters use a compact filled state. Section labels stay sentence case and neutral instead of becoming purple monospace captions. Rounded corners belong to controls, photography and a continuous functional canvas such as the Atlas map/results workspace—not to every content group.

Source and service identity uses real, allowlisted logo assets through a same-origin image proxy. Letter tiles, tier badges, decorative AI imagery, coloured selection rails, and invented partner marks are not part of the product language.

Photography and collectible artwork follow the usage and production rules in [VISUAL_ASSET_SYSTEM.md](VISUAL_ASSET_SYSTEM.md).

## Voice

Write in direct user language:

- say what the user can do now;
- name dates, cities, artists, and actions precisely;
- separate an official ticket page from an event listing;
- describe unavailable data without mentioning credentials;
- make uncertainty specific and calm.

Avoid internal reasoning and trust-performance phrases, including “why this is trusted,” “protected journey,” “source ledger,” “T1/T2,” or “API key required.” Do not imply artist endorsement, reciprocal relationships, guaranteed notification delivery, or guaranteed ticket availability.

## Localization

English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese share stable message identifiers but use independently edited copy. English is not the structural source for Chinese sentence order. Dates use the venue time zone when the user is acting on a ticket deadline; an optional local equivalent may be secondary. Product names, artist names, and official seller names preserve their official casing.
